# Dropship stores (fastchina.store)

1. **Sign up and pay**: one-time setup fee + monthly plan (Starter ₦7,500/mo + ₦20,000 setup; Growth ₦15,000/mo + ₦45,000 with a .com domain; Scale ₦35,000/mo + ₦90,000 with domain and business email). Prices are proposals.
2. **Store built**: theme, logo, colours. On Growth and Scale we register the domain **in the seller’s name**, connect DNS and issue SSL automatically. Starter gets `name.fastchina.store`.
3. **Products**: sellers add catalogue items with “+ My store”. The seller’s cost is our **retailer price** (list price minus 40% of our profit), so our profit is locked in before they sell.
4. **Selling price**: a markup rule (e.g. cost + 30%). Prices follow the yuan rate so the seller’s margin holds; prices below cost + fees are blocked.
5. **Checkout** runs on FastChina: Pay small small, Group pay and Jumia pickup stations work in every seller store.
6. **Payment split**: we keep the item cost; the seller’s margin goes to their wallet.
7. **Fulfilment**: the order joins the daily purchase list tagged with the seller, gets photo QC, ships with the seller’s branded slip and no FastChina prices.
8. **Payouts**: margin released 7 days after delivery (return window), withdrawable to any Nigerian bank. Payout speed depends on plan.
9. **Returns**: our mistake (wrong or damaged item) is on us; customer change of mind reverses the seller’s margin.
10. **Seller promotions** come out of the seller’s margin. FastChina promotions never change the seller’s cost.
11. **Lapsed plan**: 7 days’ grace, then the store is paused (not deleted). The domain stays the seller’s.

FastChina earns: setup fees, monthly fees, the profit inside every item sold, and a small margin on domains.
